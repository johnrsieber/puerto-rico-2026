## 5. Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1 — Wednesday, July 29: 🎂 Birthday Arrival
- Land at SJU at 3:47 PM, pick up Avis rental car, 10-min ride to hotel
- Check into Fairmont El San Juan
- Beach welcome toast with the crew
- **Birthday Dinner (Booked for 7:30 PM):** Caña by José Enrique (on-property, James Beard finalist)

### Day 2 — Thursday, July 30: ⛵ Private Catamaran
- Private 38-foot Lagoon 380 sailing catamaran with Captain Iván
- 8 hours, 4 guests (exclusively ours)
- **Marina:** Puerto del Rey, Fajardo (45 min drive in rental car)
- **Schedule:** Meet 8:30 AM, depart 9 AM, return 5 PM
- **Destinations:** Palomino Island or Piñeros Island (La Cordillera Nature Reserve)
- **Included:** BBQ lunch, beer, wine, homemade sangría, snorkeling gear, insurance
- **The Boat:** 1,000+ sq ft deck, trampoline, cabin, 2 bathrooms with showers
- **Captain:** USCG-certified, 4.9★ rating, 59 reviews, 175 bookings
- **Booking:** [GetMyBoat](https://www.getmyboat.com/trips/dakd43MY/)
- **Cost:** **Booked!** $2,274 total = $1,137/couple
- **What to bring:** Swimsuit underneath, change of clothes, towels, comfy footwear, sunblock, hats with chin straps
- **Dinner pick:** Cocina Abierta (Condado, global fusion, outdoor terrace)

### Day 3 — Friday, July 31: 🌿 El Yunque Rainforest
- Only tropical rainforest in the US National Forest System
- 45-minute drive east into the mountains
- **8:00 AM Arrival:** Arrive early to guarantee entry and parking.
- **Mt. Britton Tower Trail:** Drive here first to secure parking and hike while it's cooler.
- **Juan Diego Falls:** Waterfalls and pools you can swim in. Parking is very hard when full (30-min limit).
- **Yokahu Tower:** Panoramic views; on clear days, see the Virgin Islands
- **Luquillo Kiosks (Lunch):** Famous strip of 60+ beachside food vendors just outside the forest
- **Afternoon:** Head back to the hotel to clean up and relax.
- **Evening (4:00 PM - 7:00 PM):** **Sunset Walk & Taste Food Tour** with The Spoon Experience in Old San Juan. (Replaces a sit-down dinner).
- **Transport:** Drive rental car to El Yunque. Drop off car at SJU by 3:00 PM before Ubering to food tour.
- **Cost:** ~$150–$250/couple (transport, park entry, lunch)

### Day 4 — Saturday, August 1: 🏖️ Pure Beach Day
- Full day at Isla Verde Beach — resort chairs, swim-up bar, 4 pools
- Optional: rent a cabana, jet ski, parasail
- Afternoon spa at Well & Being
- **Afternoon Excursion:** Take a short Uber into Old San Juan to tour El Morro (the fort) and see the historic sights before dinner.
- **Farewell dinner:** Casita Miramar (authentic Puerto Rican comfort food, romantic vintage house, smart casual)
- **Nightlife:** Club Brava at the Fairmont (no Uber needed)

### Day 5 — Sunday, August 2: ✈️ Fly Home
- Sleep in, last breakfast on the beach
- Check out, 10-min ride to SJU
- Flight departs 3:02 PM, home by 9:40 PM

> **Important Design Decision:** The catamaran and rainforest days were **swapped** during planning. Originally the rainforest was on Thursday and catamaran on Friday. ## 7. Explore San Juan — Optional Activities

The website includes an "Explore San Juan" section with 6 activities to fill gaps between main activities. Old San Juan is only 10 min from the hotel.

1. **🏰 Castillo San Felipe del Morro** — 6-level fortress from 1500s, famous lawn for kite flying (~$10, covers both forts) — [Google Maps](https://share.google/BgMEX9h3Iae9c848q)
2. **⚔️ Castillo San Cristóbal** — Largest Spanish fortification in the New World (27 acres), "Devil's Sentry Box" — [Google Maps](https://share.google/VxPhBNB1WCu3DknrR)
3. **☂️ Calle Fortaleza (Umbrella Street)** — Most Instagrammed street in PR, colorful umbrellas overhead — [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g147320-d148185-Reviews-Calle_De_La_Fortaleza-San_Juan_Puerto_Rico.html)
4. **🌴 Paseo de la Princesa** — Tree-lined promenade along old city walls, sunset strolls, Raíces Fountain — [Google Maps](https://share.google/mAvF9L9w7CqQVZrdz)
5. **🍹 Casa Bacardí Rum Tour** — Legacy Tour, Mixology Class, or Rum Tasting. Ferry from Old San Juan Pier 2 — [bacardi.com](https://www.bacardi.com/us/en/casa-bacardi/)
6. **🎶 La Placita de Santurce** — Farmers market by day, hottest nightlife by night. Live salsa, open-air bars, dancing. Best Thu–Sat — [Google Maps](https://share.google/nbk58GM5qANQhyvKt)

**Tip on website:** Old San Juan is compact and walkable (forts + Umbrella Street + Paseo in 2–3 hours). Free trolley available. Wear comfortable shoes (cobblestones!).

## 10. Website Design & Architecture

### Technology
- **Pure HTML/CSS/JS** — single `index.html` file, separate `style.css`, no build tools or frameworks
- **No server-side code** — static site hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts — Playfair Display (headings), Inter (body), Dancing Script (logo)

### Design System
- **Theme:** Dark tropical luxury — dark navy background (`#0a1628`) with ocean blues and gold accents
- **Style:** Glassmorphism cards with backdrop blur, subtle animations
- **Color palette:**
  - `--ocean: #0077b6` / `--ocean-deep: #023e8a` / `--ocean-light: #00b4d8`
  - `--gold: #f4a261` (accent/highlights)
  - `--sunset: #f77f00` / `--sunset-pink: #e63946`
  - `--night: #0a1628` (background)
  - Glass: `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)` with `rgba(255,255,255,0.2)` borders

### Page Structure (sections in order)
1. **Navigation** — fixed, glassmorphic, with hamburger menu on mobile
2. **Hero** — full-viewport background image with animated text
3. **Flights** — boarding-pass style glass cards with routing info
4. **Hotel** — hero image, amenity grid, room comparison table
5. **Itinerary** — vertical timeline with day-by-day cards and photos
6. **Costs** — grid layout with totals and upgrade callout
7. **Dining** — glass cards for each night's recommendation with backup options
8. **Explore** — 6-card grid of Old San Juan activities
9. **Good to Know** — tip cards grid + booking order steps
10. **CTA** — simple "Ready to Say Yes?" section (no button)
11. **Footer** — minimal

### Key CSS Details
- Timeline card images: 700px tall (`object-fit: cover`)
- Hotel hero image: 400px tall
- Mobile breakpoint: 768px
- Animations: `fadeUp`, `fadeDown`, `bounce`, scroll-triggered `fade-in` via IntersectionObserver
