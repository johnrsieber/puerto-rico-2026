Friday, July 31
🌿 El Yunque Rainforest
Adventure Nature Waterfall

The only tropical rainforest in the US National Forest System. A 45-minute drive east into the mountains for a day of waterfalls, ancient trees, and panoramic views.

  • 8:00 AM Arrival — Arrive right as the gates open to guarantee entry and parking.
  • Mt. Britton Tower Trail (First) — Drive here first to secure parking and do this longer hike up into the mystical cloud forest while it's still cool.
  • Juan Diego Falls — A series of beautiful natural pools and waterfalls you can wade and swim in. (Parking here is very difficult once the park fills up and has a strict 30-minute limit).
  • Yokahu Tower — Panoramic views of the forest canopy and Caribbean coast. On clear days, see the Virgin Islands.
  • Luquillo Kiosks (Lunch) — Famous strip of 60+ beachside food vendors just outside the forest for local food and cold Medallas.
  • Back at resort by mid-afternoon for pool time

💡 Transport & Parking: We need to rent a car for the day (Ubers cannot pick up inside the forest). Arriving early is critical to beat the heat and the crowds!

Est. cost: ~$150–$250/couple (transport, park entry, lunch)

If We Have Time

Explore San Juan 🏰

Old San Juan is only 10 minutes from the hotel — a walkable, colorful, 500-year-old colonial city packed with history, forts, and culture. Here are some things we could check out between activities.

🏰

Castillo San Felipe del Morro

Massive 6-level fortress at the tip of Old San Juan, built in the 1500s to defend against sea attacks. The expansive lawn outside is famous for kite flying with stunning ocean views.

~$10 entry · 9 AM–5 PM · Covers both forts

⚔️

Castillo San Cristóbal

The largest fortification built by the Spanish in the New World — 27 acres. Features the legendary "Devil's Sentry Box" (Garita del Diablo) with its mysterious legends.

Same ticket as El Morro

☂️

Calle Fortaleza (Umbrella Street)

One of the most Instagrammed streets in Puerto Rico — colorful umbrellas and art installations suspended overhead. Walk the blue cobblestone streets lined with shops and galleries.

🌴

Paseo de la Princesa

Beautiful tree-lined promenade along the old city walls. Perfect for a sunset stroll with views of San Juan Bay and the Raíces Fountain. Often has street vendors and live music.

🍹

Casa Bacardí Rum Tour

Just across the bay from Old San Juan. Choose from a Legacy Tour, Mixology Class, or Rum Tasting. Includes welcome cocktails. Take the scenic ferry from Old San Juan Pier 2.

bacardi.com · Book in advance

🎶

La Placita de Santurce

By day it's a farmers market; by night it transforms into San Juan's hottest nightlife scene. Live salsa music, open-air bars, dancing in the streets. Best on Thursday–Saturday nights.

📌 Good to know: Old San Juan is compact and walkable — you can hit the forts, Umbrella Street, and Paseo de la Princesa in a 2–3 hour stroll. A free trolley runs through the historic district if your legs need a break. Wear comfortable shoes (cobblestones!) and bring sunscreen.
